I'm a full-time founder and AI product manager based in Pune, India. After five years in enterprise product roles — including leading digital product initiatives at TVS Motor Company — I left to build products at the frontier of AI.

I founded AIAgentStudio.ai, a no-code visual AI agent builder, built in TypeScript from a blank repo to production — visual canvas, AI co-founder chat, agent marketplace, and India-first integrations. I've shipped 9 live products across AI SaaS, telehealth, health e-commerce, travel, and messaging.

I'm a Perplexity AI Business Fellow, and my AI learning platform AILearnX.com was selected as part of that fellowship. I also built MedPe.in, an India-first telehealth platform bringing doctor consultations online with Razorpay-native payments.

I hold an MBA from TU Nürnberg, Germany and a B.E. in Instrumentation Engineering from the University of Pune. I've consulted internationally at ITQ GmbH in Germany and Las Palmas, Spain.
