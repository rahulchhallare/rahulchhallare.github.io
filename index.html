aiagentstudio.ai
🤖aiagentstudio.ai
AI SaaS · No-Code · India-first · Seed Stage
AIAgentStudio: No-Code Visual AI Agent Builder
Problem
Non-technical founders couldn't build AI agents without deep engineering resources — costly developers or US-centric integrations that didn't work in India.
Approach
Visual drag-and-drop canvas with AI co-founder chat that scaffolds agent logic from plain English. India-first: Razorpay, WhatsApp Business API, GST APIs, Tally. Agent template marketplace with creator revenue-share via Stripe Connect.
Metrics I'd Measure
Agent activation rate, template marketplace GMV, MRR growth vs. 00K target, NPS from India SMB cohort.

Telehealth · India · Healthtech · Consumer
MedPe: Telehealth Platform for Bharat
Problem
Indian patients lacked affordable, trustworthy access to online doctor consultations with legally compliant digital prescriptions.
Approach
Privacy-first platform with instant slot booking, UPI-native checkout, digital prescriptions and specialist referral flow. PDPB compliant from day one.
Metrics I'd Measure
Consultation completion rate, repeat visit rate, prescription download rate, UPI payment success rate, CAC vs. LTV.

🚁bookmyhelicopter.in
B2G · Marketplace · Travel India · Maharashtra
BookMyHelicopter: B2G Aggregator for Kumbh Mela 2027
Problem
No neutral platform aggregated helicopter operators across India's pilgrimage, corporate, tourism, and emergency verticals.
Approach
Used Kumbh Mela 2027 as a forcing function — pitched to the Authority, structured Maharashtra Govt. EOI, signed 8+ operators. Consumer bookings as parallel revenue while B2G pipeline matures.
Metrics I'd Measure
Operator GMV, Kumbh 2027 booking volume, government contract value, consumer repeat booking rate.

Android · Consumer · Messaging · Made in India
InstaChat: Secure Messenger — 1K+ Downloads, 4.2★

instahealthymart.com
🥗instahealthymart.com
E-Commerce · D2C · India Wellness · Consumer
InstaHealthyMart: Chemical-Free Wellness D2C Platform
Problem
India's health-conscious consumers had no trusted, curated D2C source for chemical-free wellness products — greenwashing, opaque ingredients, fragmented discovery.
Approach
Full D2C stack: curated catalogue, cart, checkout, fulfilment. Razorpay, UPI, and COD for maximum checkout conversion. Drop-shipping model to manage inventory risk.
Metrics I'd Measure
Checkout conversion rate, COD vs. prepaid ratio, repeat purchase rate, cart abandonment rate, return rate.
